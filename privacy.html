Privacy Policy

Version 2026-08-01 · Last updated August 1, 2026

In plain English: we store your username, email, a scrambled version of your password, and your game progress. We use your email to confirm your account and to reach you about your account. We do not sell your data. You can ask us to delete your account and we will.

This policy explains what Afterlife Online (“the Game”) collects, why, and what you can do about it. Afterlife Online is operated by Net Artisan Collective (“we”, “us”).

What we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details — your username, your email address, and your password. Your password is stored only as a salted Argon2 hash; we never store or have access to the password itself.
  • Consent choices — the date and time you accepted these terms, which version you accepted, and whether you opted in to project news.
  • Bug reports — if you submit one from inside the Game, we receive your description and (if you include it) a screenshot of your game window, along with your character name, location, client version, and operating system. Only send a screenshot you're comfortable sharing.

Information created by playing

  • Character and progress data — your characters, their stats, inventory, position, quests, guild membership, mail, auctions, and similar game state.
  • Chat and social data — messages you send in public, party, guild, or private channels, your friends and ignore lists, and guild bank activity. Some of this is retained so the Game works and so we can investigate abuse reports.

Information collected automatically

  • Connection data — your IP address and connection timestamps, which are inherent to connecting to any online service. We use these to run the Game, to diagnose problems, and to detect and stop abuse (cheating, spam, denial-of-service).
  • Client version and platform — so the Game can tell you when an update is required.

We do not use advertising trackers, third-party analytics scripts, or cross-site tracking cookies on our website or in the Game client.

Email we send you

There are two kinds, and they are separate:

  • Account email (confirmation codes, security notices). You cannot opt out of these while you have an account — they are how the account works.
  • Project news. Only if you ticked the optional box when registering. Every such email includes an unsubscribe link, and you can also ask us to turn it off at any time using the contact address below. We will never add you to this without you asking.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for anyone else's advertising. We use a small number of service providers to run the Game:

How long we keep it

  • Account and character data — for as long as your account exists. If you ask us to delete your account, we remove it from the live database.
  • Backups — our database backups rotate on roughly an eight-day cycle, so deleted data can persist in a backup for up to about eight days before it ages out.
  • Bug reports — kept while the issue is open and for a reasonable period afterward.
  • Connection logs — kept only as long as useful for diagnostics and abuse prevention.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you may ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data we hold about you, and give you a copy;
  • correct data that is wrong;
  • delete your account and its data;
  • stop sending you project news (or stop other processing based on consent);
  • object to, or ask us to restrict, processing based on legitimate interests.

Email us at the address below and we will act on your request. We will not treat you differently for exercising these rights. If you are in the EU/UK and you think we have handled your data badly, you also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Security

Passwords are stored as Argon2 hashes, never as text. The connection between the Game client and our server is encrypted, and traffic to our website and download servers uses HTTPS. Confirmation codes are stored hashed, expire after 30 minutes, and lock out after repeated wrong guesses. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a password you don't use anywhere else.

Children

Afterlife Online is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy materially, we will update the version and date at the top of this page and ask for fresh agreement inside the Game where appropriate. The version you agreed to is recorded with your account.

Contact

Questions, or want your data or account deleted? Email support@afterlife-online.com. We are Net Artisan Collective, operators of Afterlife Online.
